## Enemy walk-animation speed tuning (18 Jul)
- Walk playback is synced to ground speed by `UnitAnimator` (MoveSpeed = groundSpeed / walkClipNaturalSpeed). Two artistic knobs on top: **per-enemy** `EnemyV4_*.asset → Walk Anim Speed Multiplier` (0.25–2, default 1 — each enemy owns its value in its own config) and **global** `GameConfig.asset → Enemy Walk Anim Speed Multiplier` (0.25–2, default 1 — master over all enemies). Effective factor = per-enemy × global. 1 = feet synced; <1 = calmer legs (slight slide), >1 = busier. Skeleton1/2 ship at 0.7 (their fast moveSpeed made a synced walk read frantic).
